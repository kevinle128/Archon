## Codebase Patterns


### isInterruptMarkedResult (provider-neutral)
- **Where**: `packages/workflows/src/dag-executor.ts` (beside INTERRUPT_TERMINAL_REASONS)
- **Pattern**: Claude terminalReason allowlist OR exact DeepSeek triple (`stopReason==='aborted' && isError===true && errorSubtype==='deepseek_aborted'`). No provider-id branching. Call sites still require live token + wasOperatorInterrupted.
- **Block gate**: product `DEEPSEEK_CAPABILITIES.interrupt` stays false until spike Proceed; conformance fixture opts the fake provider into `'native'` only for tests (instance caps preferred over registry when declared).

### DeepSeek dual-signal cancel (requestCancel + flushCancel)
- **Where**: `packages/providers/src/community/deepseek/acp-client.ts` (`driveDeepseekAcpTurn`)
- **Pattern**: Shared turn state outside listeners. `requestCancel(cause)` records FIRST cause (`node-cancel` | `operator-interrupt` | `cleanup`) and releases a local deferred once. `flushCancel()` is a no-op until `clientCtx` + `activeSessionId` exist, then sends/memoizes one `session/cancel`. Pre-session cancels stay pending. `promptSettledNaturally` freezes outcome so a late Stop during `session/close` cannot convert a natural result or send stale cancel.
- **Example**: Match Devin's `CANCEL_DRAIN_GRACE_MS = 500` via `Promise.race([prompt, localCancellation.then(waitMs)])`.

### Pinned-runtime spikes stay out of CI
- **Where**: `packages/providers/src/community/deepseek/interrupt-resume-spike.ts`, `package.json` `spike:interrupt:deepseek`
- **Pattern**: Require `DEEPSEEK_LIVE_TEST=1` + credentials; disposable temp cwd always removed; version-check pins; emit sanitized JSON only (equality booleans, no prompts/secrets/session ids); write evidence under `plans/reports/`.
