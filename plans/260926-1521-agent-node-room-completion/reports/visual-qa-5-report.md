## Method

- **Build.** All nine qa4-fixes commits are ancestors of HEAD (`0d24f4fd`, `5f03603a`, `730f2c21`, `80ef8c54`, `b0d27be3`, `b0a4e12e`, `bf789202`, `af5166d3`, `b566191a`). - **Isolated server.** The DB was copied with `sqlite3 ~/.archon/archon.db ".backup …"`. `config.yaml` and `credential-key` were copied into a scratch `ARCHON_HOME`. The only config change was `assistants.claude.advisorModel: opus`, made before the first boot. The server ran on 3339 with the env line from the brief, and Vite ran on 5207. Every worktree is under the scratch home.
- **Workflows.** The round-4 set was reused under the `vq5-` prefix.
- **Providers.** Every run used a real subscription. No fake provider was used.
- **Capture.** One Playwright context at 1440×900 with `X-Archon-User: vq5-operator`. It had three tabs: Console, Legacy and mockup. The panels were measured at Console **520 px** (`console-inspect-room`) and Legacy **460 px** (`legacy-node-room`).
- **Mockups.** The mockup files are unchanged (mtime 2026-09-26 15:13). All 32 state crops (8 × {prompt, loop} × {Console, Legacy}) were recaptured fresh from `http://127.0.0.1:8791/` in the `codex · queue only` transport, cropped to the mockup `aside` (520 / 460 px). Full pages of `Transcript States` and `Steering Dock States` were also captured.
- **Pairs.** `pair-*.png` puts the mockup (or the other shell) on the left and the app on the right.
- **Timing.** Transients were sampled from the DOM of both tabs every 40–100 ms.
- **Evidence.** Everything is in `plans/260926-1521-agent-node-room-completion/evidence/visual-qa-5/`.
